# Evidence scope and interpretation

Reviewed on 23 September 2026 for BPSC CCE General Studies. The rolling window
starts 23 September 2016. The relevant prelim cycles are 60th–62nd (2017), 63rd
(2018), 64th (2018), 65th (2019), 66th (2020), valid 67th re-exam (2022), 68th
(2023), 69th (2023), 70th (2024), and 71st (2025). GS1 and GS2 mains examples
are included for the same cycles, including the 71st mains in 2026. CCE cycles
are irregular; this is not ten annual examinations. No 72nd prelim mock or
future paper is counted.

## What was checked

Read native text and OCR of downloaded booklet copies and publisher
transcriptions. Inspect page images when OCR merges columns or loses numbers.
Record a concise question cue, question number, one-based PDF page, cycle,
stage, thematic pattern, classification and source ID for each selected example.
Keep SHA-256 fingerprints and URLs for all 30 referenced documents. Question
numbering is specific to the linked copy; prelim sets differ.

Official BPSC archive endpoints returned retrieval errors during this review.
Consequently, the evidence comes from public mirrors of exam booklets and
explicitly labelled publisher transcriptions. The 60th–62nd, 63rd, 64th, 65th
and 66th prelim evidence uses transcriptions; later prelim and mains sources
are booklet mirrors. This is not a claim of independent authentication of every
mirror or transcription. Prefer an official copy when it becomes retrievable.
Official archive starting points:

- https://bpsc.bihar.gov.in/question-booklets/
- https://bpsc.bihar.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Syllabus-CC-Exam-Updated.pdf
- https://bpsc.bihar.gov.in/Archive/2022/NB-2022-11-17-02.pdf

An index linked the cancelled May 2022 67th paper under a September re-exam
label. That paper was excluded. The ledger uses the September re-exam Set D
copy, code 24/FG-AC/CC/PT-2022/2-D. The 70th January 2025 re-exam was checked as
supplementary context, but is not an extra cycle or included in the ledger.
Other special sittings and all alternative sets were not exhaustively audited.

There are 198 selected examples, including individual mains subquestions.
The sample demonstrates transferable question patterns; it does not estimate
topic proportions. Do not turn sample counts into historical exam weightage,
an accuracy score, or a probability that an article will be asked. No answer
key adjudication was performed. Ignore handwritten option marks and publisher
solutions when selecting evidence. A question cue records what was asked,
not a claim that every premise in the question or its options is correct.

## Classifications

| Label | Meaning | Use in selection |
|---|---|---|
| `dated` | A named event, current office-holder, release, reporting period or milestone | Preserve specific factual briefs and recent developments |
| `current_linked` | A concept, institution or continuing scheme associated with current affairs | Support useful explainers; do not count it as a dated event |
| `applied_mains` | Policy evaluation, causes, effects, significance or practical application | Retain analysis and examples that can inform a mains answer |

Ordinary static history/geography and artificial statistical exercises are not
evidence of current-affairs frequency. A history or science explainer can still
qualify when linked to an actual current issue and specifically labelled as such.

## Findings translated into rules

| Observed pattern | Source-located examples | Filtering consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Programme objective, beneficiary or features | 60th–62nd prelim Q150; 67th prelim Q61; 71st prelim Q26–27 | Keep scheme briefs and detailed implementation reports |
| Bihar economic data and development | 64th prelim Q98/Q100; 70th prelim Q91/Q98; 71st prelim Q28/Q30 | Give Bihar budgets, surveys, district facts and infrastructure high priority |
| Books, awards and appointments | 60th–62nd prelim Q55/Q78; 67th prelim Q131; 71st prelim Q3/Q9 | Do not discard small cultural and award briefs as automatically irrelevant |
| Sport, hosts, firsts and themes | 63rd prelim Q31; 67th prelim Q138; 70th prelim Q35/Q89 | Retain major milestones and Bihar achievers; exclude routine match chatter |
| Missions, terms and applied science | 63rd prelim Q34; 69th prelim Q1/Q6; 71st prelim Q146 | Preserve agency, payload, mechanism, abbreviation and application details |
| Reports and policy concepts | 69th prelim Q34/Q39; 65th GS1 Q8; 69th GS1 Q4d | Retain publisher/year/finding and explanatory context |
| International developments | 63rd prelim Q36; 67th prelim Q145; 68th GS1 Q6a | World affairs can qualify without an explicit India connection |
| Mains evaluation of active policy | 67th GS1 Q8–10; 68th GS1 Q5a–b; 70th GS1 Q5a–6b | Keep causes, constitutional issues, trade-offs and implementation evidence |
| Culture linked to development | 68th prelim Q85; 71st GS1 Q6a | Retain GI/heritage facts and substantive cultural analysis |

High versus standard priority is an editorial choice, not fitted statistical
weightage. Both priorities are included; priority is never a quota or a reason
to suppress all awards/sports. The runtime accepts a specific syllabus extension
for a new issue even without a historical match, and records that distinction.

## Refreshing the evidence

For a new completed CCE cycle, obtain the valid booklet, record set/sitting,
hash and locators, and classify examples consistently. Verify cancellations or
re-exams before adding evidence. Do not silently replace old references or use
coaching frequency tables as measured results. Change the policy version and
deploy the updated runtime snapshot after validation. A future full statistical
study would require an explicit classification of every question, treatment of
overlapping categories and disputed cases, and a declared denominator.
