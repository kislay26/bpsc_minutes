## How the system uses this evidence

- Retain an article if it supplies a useful prelims fact or a mains analytical angle, with a concrete current hook.
- Match the transferable question pattern and cite its ledger ID internally; do not imply the newspaper article itself was previously asked.
- Give substantive Bihar content extra priority and preserve significant short award, book and sports briefs.
- Permit clearly identified syllabus extensions for new issues; exclude routine noise and do not impose quotas.
- Publish only original newspaper cuttings. Keep generated selection analysis in local audit files.
